## 3) Product Hunt (EN)

**Name:** ŞİMDİ (necaliyor.co)

**Tagline (60 karakter altı):**
> See what's playing right now on Turkish & world radio

**Description:**
> ŞİMDİ shows what's playing *right now* across 124 radio stations — Turkish and
> a curated set of world stations (KEXP, FIP, Radio Swiss Jazz, SomaFM…), live.
> Every "now" is kept in a permanent archive, so a song is never lost. Discover
> stations by mood, watch the "Pulse" of what's playing on multiple stations at
> once, and share a live card of whatever's on. No ads, free, and made with a
> poet's touch.

**First comment (maker):**
> Hi PH 👋 I'm Berkay. In an age where I can stream anything on demand, I kept
> coming back to radio for one reason: it brings me what I *didn't* choose — and
> the song playing for me is playing for a stranger somewhere too. I couldn't
> find a place to see all of it at once, so I built one. It grew from a weekend
> project into something a little more literary. Would love your thoughts. 🎧

**Topics:** Music, Radio, Web App
